# Briefcase

> AI-native accounting automation. From invoice capture to financial close.

Briefcase automates bookkeeping workflows for UK accounting firms and businesses using AI agents. Upload invoices, receipts, and bank statements — Briefcase extracts data, categorises transactions, handles VAT, and publishes to Xero or QuickBooks.
